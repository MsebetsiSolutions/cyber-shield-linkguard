Serious cyber crimes

Important: Always keep evidence (screenshots, URLs, etc.) when reporting malicious content. Provide as much detail as possible to help authorities take action.

Reporting Guidelines

1
Gather Evidence

Take screenshots, save URLs, and document timestamps.

2
Be Specific

Provide clear details about what you observed.

3
Include Context

Explain how you encountered the content.

4
Follow Up

Keep records of your report and follow up.

Best Practices

  • Use encrypted communication when sharing sensitive information
  • Keep copies of all correspondence with authorities
  • Report promptly to increase chances of successful intervention
  • Be patient - investigations can take time
