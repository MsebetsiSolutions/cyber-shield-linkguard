👣 Your Digital Footprint

Everything you do online leaves a trail called a digital footprint. This includes searches, posts, and likes. Just like real footprints, digital footprints can last a long time!

Digital Footprint Trail Permanent

🎣 Phishing Fishermen

Phishing is when bad guys try to trick you into giving them personal information. They might send fake emails or messages that look real. Be careful with links from people you don't know!

🛡️ The Shield of Privacy Settings

Games and apps have special settings called privacy settings that control who sees your information. You're in charge of your privacy!

Privacy Settings Control Information Sharing

💎 Guardians of the Data

Personal data is information about you like your name, address, and birthday. Companies want this information, so it's important to protect it!
