🔗 Link Safety Inspector

Before clicking any link, you should inspect it. Enter a URL to analyze:

Safe Link Checking Tips:

  • Hover over links to see the real URL (don't click!)
  • Check for misspellings in domain names (paypa1 vs paypal)
  • Be suspicious of shortened URLs (bit.ly, tinyurl)
  • Look for HTTPS and legitimate domain extensions
  • Watch out for extra characters or unusual subdomains
