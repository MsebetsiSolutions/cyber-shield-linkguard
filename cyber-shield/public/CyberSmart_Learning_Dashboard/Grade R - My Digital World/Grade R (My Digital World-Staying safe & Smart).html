Our Philosophy

In today's connected world, children are "digital natives." Our curriculum doesn't aim to scare them away from technology but to empower them to use it safely, responsibly, and confidently. We focus on building "Smart Thinking" habits, just like we teach them to look both ways before crossing the street.

🌟 Educator Implementation Tips

Use the African characters as puppets or props during lessons to make concepts more engaging
Connect digital safety concepts to traditional African wisdom and proverbs
Involve parents and grandparents in the learning process - they're natural wisdom keepers
Create physical "secret code" cards with African patterns for password practice
Use call-and-response techniques common in African learning traditions
Celebrate "Digital Safety Champions" with small African-themed certificates

Module 4: Digital Safari Signals

Core Concept: Learning to recognize online safety signals, just like we recognize animal tracks in the wild.

🎨 Activity: Safari Signals

Learn the Digital Safari Signals:

  • GREEN - Safe path (educational games, trusted videos)
  • YELLOW - Caution needed (pop-ups, strange requests)
  • RED - Danger ahead (scary content, mean messages)

Smart Thinking Rule #2

If something online makes you feel yellow or red, stop what you're doing and tell a trusted adult right away, just like you would on a real safari.

Story Time: Zawadi's Yellow Feeling

Zawadi was playing a learning game when a pop-up appeared asking for her name. She remembered the safari signals and felt this was a yellow light. She stopped and showed her big sister, who was proud of her for recognizing the caution signal!

Game: Thumbs Up, Thumbs Down

Decide what you should do in each situation!
