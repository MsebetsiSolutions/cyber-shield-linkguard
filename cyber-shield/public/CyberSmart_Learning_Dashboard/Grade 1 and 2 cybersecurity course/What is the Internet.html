🌐 What is the Internet?

The Giant Invisible Spider Web

The Internet is like a giant invisible spider web that connects computers and devices all over the world. It helps people share information, send messages, watch videos, and play games.

Here's what you need to know about the Internet:

  • Think of it as a network of invisible roads connecting computers all around the world
  • Messages and pictures travel along these roads very quickly, almost instantly!
  • Without the Internet, websites, emails, and online games wouldn't work - it's what makes everything connected
  • When you send a message or watch a video, it travels through this invisible network to reach you

Real World Example:

Sending a message to a friend far away is like sending a paper airplane through a long tunnel of roads—it reaches the other side almost instantly! The Internet makes this magic happen by connecting devices together in a giant web.

How to Connect Devices:
