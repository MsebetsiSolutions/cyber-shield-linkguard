❤️ Being Kind Online

The Digital Golden Rule

How do you feel when someone shares a toy with you? Or says, "I like your drawing!"? It feels great!

Now, how do you feel if someone says something mean or won't let you play? It doesn't feel good at all.

The internet is not just a bunch of screens—it's a place full of real people with real feelings. The same rules of kindness apply here! Always treat others online the way you would want to be treated. This is what it means to be a good Digital Citizen.

🚫 What is Cyberbullying?

Sometimes, people use phones, tablets, or games to be mean to others on purpose. This is called cyberbullying.

It can look like:

  • Sending a mean message
  • Leaving someone out of an online game on purpose
  • Saying something unkind about someone's post or picture

🛡️ What Can You Do?

If you see or experience something unkind online, remember your superpower plan: Stop, Block, and Tell!

1. STOP - Don't answer mean messages. Don't write something mean back.

2. BLOCK - Most games and apps have a "block" button. This is like building a magic wall so that person can't send you any more messages. Ask a parent to help you find it.

3. TELL - This is the most important step! Always tell a trusted adult—like your mom, dad, grandma, grandpa, or teacher—what happened. They can help you.
