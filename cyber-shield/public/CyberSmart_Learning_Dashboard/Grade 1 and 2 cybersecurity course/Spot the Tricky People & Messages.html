⚠️ Spot the Tricky People & Messages

Stay Safe from Online Tricks

Some people online try to trick you. These could be messages saying "You won a prize!" or strangers asking you for your information.

Here's how to stay safe online:

  • Always ask yourself: "Does this message seem strange or too good to be true?" If something seems suspicious, it probably is!
  • Never share personal information like your full name, address, phone number, or passwords with anyone online, even if they seem nice
  • Remember the golden rule: "Stop, Don't Reply, Tell an Adult" whenever you see something that makes you uncomfortable
  • Only talk to people you know in real life when you're playing games or using apps online
  • If someone asks for your password or personal information, that's a big red flag - never share it!

🚨 EMERGENCY RULE 🚨

STOP - DON'T REPLY - TELL AN ADULT

Remember this rule whenever you feel unsure about a message online!

Real World Example:

If someone in a game you don't know says, "Send me your password and I'll give you coins," it's definitely a trick! You should immediately stop talking to them, don't reply to their message, and tell a parent or teacher what happened. Real friends would never ask for your password!

CoolGameFriend99

Class Discussion: "What will you do if someone you don't know messages you online?"

Remember: It's always better to be safe than sorry. If you're ever unsure about a message or person online, Stop - Don't Reply - Tell an Adult! Your safety is the most important thing.
