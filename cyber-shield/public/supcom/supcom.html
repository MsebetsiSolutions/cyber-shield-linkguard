Getting Started

To get started, simply navigate to the Scanner Dashboard and use the scan options available. You can scan URLs, files, or QR codes to check for potential threats. No complex setup required!

LinkGuard is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means you can install it on your mobile device directly from your browser. Look for the "Install" option in your browser menu.

Scanning Features

Our scanner detects various threats including malware, phishing websites, suspicious files, and malicious QR codes for comprehensive security analysis.

Scan results are highly accurate as we leverage extensive database of known threats. However, no security solution is 100% foolproof, so always exercise caution online.
