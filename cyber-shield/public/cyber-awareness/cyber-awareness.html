Global Phishing Patterns by Region

Learn about different phishing tactics used around the world to better protect yourself

🌍

Africa

419 Scams
Common Patterns:
  • Nigerian Prince scams
  • Lottery winnings notifications
  • Inheritance claims
  • Charity/humanitarian appeals
  • Investment opportunities
Example Subject:
"URGENT: Claim Your $2.5M Inheritance"
Often use emotional appeals and urgency
🇺🇸

USA

Corporate Impersonation
Common Patterns:
  • Bank security alerts
  • IRS tax notifications
  • Amazon/PayPal account issues
  • Social Security suspensions
  • COVID-19 relief scams
Example Subject:
"Action Required: Your Account Will Be Suspended"
Mimic trusted institutions and government agencies
🇮🇳

India

Tech Support
Common Patterns:
  • Fake tech support calls
  • KYC verification scams
  • UPI/digital wallet fraud
  • Job opportunity scams
  • Educational loan offers
Example Subject:
"Complete Your KYC or Account Will Be Blocked"
Target digital payment users and students
🇷🇺

Russia

Advanced Persistent
Common Patterns:
  • Business email compromise
  • Fake software updates
  • Government service impersonation
  • Cryptocurrency scams
  • Romance scams
Example Subject:
"Critical Security Update Required"
Often sophisticated and well-researched
