Msebetsi Business Logic: Comparison Operators
Activity Overview
Title: Decision Making with Comparison Operators
Duration: 35-45 minutes
Learning Objectives:

Understand and apply comparison operators (==, ===, !=, !==, >, <, >=, <=)

Differentiate between loose (==) and strict (===) equality

Use comparisons in business decision making

Combine multiple conditions with logical operators

Learning Progression:
Basic Comparisons: == vs ===, truth table completion

Business Logic: Building decision-making functions

Debugging: Finding and fixing comparison errors

Real Applications: Complete business systems

Assessment Criteria:
✅ Correct use of comparison operators

✅ Understanding of loose vs strict equality

✅ Ability to build business logic functions

✅ Debugging and error-finding skills

✅ Real-world application understanding

This comprehensive lesson package provides everything needed to teach comparison operators effectively within the Msebetsi business context!
