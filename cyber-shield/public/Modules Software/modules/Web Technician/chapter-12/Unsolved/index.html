🚀 Innovation

Kenya's Tech Hub

Nairobi's "Silicon Savannah" has attracted over $1 billion in tech investment since 2015, making it Africa's leading startup ecosystem outside of South Africa 🇰🇪.

Region: East Africa Growth: 22% annually
📱 Mobile

Mobile Money Revolution

M-Pesa processes more transactions in Kenya than Western Union does globally, with over 50 million active users across 7 African countries 💸.

Users: 50M+ Countries: 7
🔋 Infrastructure

Renewable Energy Boom

Africa added more renewable energy capacity in 2023 than North America and Europe combined, with solar leading the growth at 45% increase year-over-year ☀️.

Growth: 45% YoY Leader: Solar
👩‍💻 Talent

Developer Community

Africa has the world's fastest-growing developer population, with Nigeria, Egypt, and South Africa producing 50% of the continent's tech talent 💻.

Growth: 3.8% monthly Top 3: NG, EG, ZA
🏦 FinTech

Digital Banking

57% of African adults now use digital financial services, compared to just 23% in 2014 – a 148% increase in one decade 📈.

Adoption: 57% Growth: 148%
🌍 Connectivity

Internet Penetration

Africa's internet users grew by 20% in 2023, reaching 570 million people – that's more than the entire population of the European Union 🌐.

Users: 570M Growth: 20%
