Important: Always keep evidence (screenshots, URLs, etc.) when reporting malicious content. Provide as much detail as possible to help authorities take action.

Reporting Guidelines

1
Gather Evidence

Take screenshots, save URLs, and document timestamps of the malicious content.

2
Be Specific

Provide clear details about what you observed and why it appears malicious.

3
Include Context

Explain how you encountered the content and any related information.

4
Follow Up

Keep records of your report and follow up if you don't receive confirmation.

Best Practices

  • Use encrypted communication when sharing sensitive information
  • Keep copies of all correspondence with authorities
  • Report promptly to increase chances of successful intervention
  • Be patient - investigations can take time
